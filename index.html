WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

Creating Opportunities. Inspiring Change.

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organization working towards community development and social empowerment.

The organization focuses on creating opportunities for underprivileged communities through education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare and community support.

Through different initiatives and campaigns, InAmigos aims to encourage people to come together and contribute towards positive social change.
